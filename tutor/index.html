How it works

From slide deck to lecture, in four steps.

  1. 01

    Upload your slides

    Add a lecture deck as a PDF to a course. The text of every slide is extracted and its language is detected.

  2. 02

    Claude analyzes the deck

    The whole deck is read at once to work out the topic, its scope, the key terms and the background it assumes.

  3. 03

    It researches and writes

    Claude researches the topic on the web, then writes one cohesive lecture across all the slides. Every source it used is kept, so you can see what the lecture is based on.

  4. 04

    Listen or watch

    Google text-to-speech voices the narration slide by slide, as an audio lecture or a video of your slides with the narration.

Features

Built for studying, not just summarizing.

Lecture generation

A researched, narrated lecture built from your own course slides, with the web sources behind it listed next to the lecture.

Supervised problem-solving chat

Upload a problem set or exam and work through it in a chat. A main agent solves step by step; a second supervisor agent solves it again independently and compares. Every answer is marked:

  • verified by supervisor
  • corrected by supervisor
  • ⚠ uncertain — verify with your course material

Multilingual

AI Tutor narrates and chats in the student’s language, for example Hebrew, English or Russian. Lectures can be voiced in another language than the slides, and the chat answers in the language you write in.
